# MaggioFlix – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

MaggioFlix is a private app for watching and requesting content on the MaggioFlix home media server. It is offered free of charge to people who have an account on that server. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## What the app handles

- **Sign-in data.** When you sign in, the app receives an access token from Plex (plex.tv) and a session from the server's request service (Seerr). Tokens are stored only on your device, in the system's secure storage.
- **Server data.** Your libraries, watch progress, watch history and requests are read from and sent to the MaggioFlix server you are signed in to, so the app can show and update them.
- **Download progress and notifications.** The app asks the MaggioFlix server for the status of requested titles and shows local notifications on your device when they become available.
- **Settings and cache.** Preferences, artwork and metadata are stored on your device to make the app faster.

## What the app does not do

- It does not show ads.
- It does not include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
- The developer does not receive your data. It only goes to the MaggioFlix server, which is run by its administrator.

## Third-party services

- **Plex** (plex.tv) is used to sign in and to reach the media server. Plex's own privacy policy applies to your Plex account.
- **Artwork** (posters, backgrounds) is loaded from public image services such as The Movie Database (TMDB) and TheTVDB. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when an image is downloaded.

All connections use encryption (HTTPS) where the server supports it.

## Deleting your data

Signing out or uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device. To delete your account and the data kept on the MaggioFlix server (watch history, requests), ask the server administrator.

## Children

MaggioFlix is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address. For questions, open an issue on the project page: https://github.com/vinceslao17/maggioflixtv/issues

# MaggioFlix – Informativa sulla privacy (italiano)

MaggioFlix è un'app privata per guardare e richiedere contenuti sul server MaggioFlix, gratuita per chi ha un account su quel server.

- **Dati trattati:** il token di accesso di Plex e la sessione di Seerr, salvati solo sul dispositivo; libreria, avanzamento, cronologia e richieste, scambiati soltanto con il server MaggioFlix; impostazioni e copertine in cache sul dispositivo; notifiche locali quando un contenuto è disponibile.
- **Cosa non fa:** niente pubblicità, niente analisi o tracciamento, niente segnalazioni automatiche di crash. I dati non vengono venduti né condivisi, e lo sviluppatore non li riceve.
- **Servizi esterni:** Plex (plex.tv) per l'accesso, con la sua informativa; TMDB e TheTVDB per le immagini, che vedono l'indirizzo IP quando le scarichi.
- **Cancellazione:** uscire dall'account o disinstallare l'app cancella i dati sul dispositivo. Per cancellare account e dati sul server, chiedi all'amministratore.
- **Contatti:** https://github.com/vinceslao17/maggioflixtv/issues
